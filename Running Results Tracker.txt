max_active_drawdown is a running-peak difference on a cumsum of overlapping
5-day active returns, so it is in summed-return units. Published values ran
-1.03 to -1.78. In capital terms the same strategies draw down 52 to 59
percent absolute, 26 to 46 percent active.

VENDOR CONSTRAINT DISCOVERED
FiinQuant serves only the last 3650 days. The canonical history starts
2016-07-26 and was downloaded 2026-07-24, the last day that date was inside
the window. A full refetch now fails with TimeFrameLimitFailed, so the local
files are the only copy of the early history. download_fiinquant_history.py
writes only what it fetched with no merge, so narrowing its --from-date would
have replaced ten years with the fetched slice. scripts/
extend_fiinquant_history.py merges instead, and rescales stored adjusted
prices by the measured per-ticker restatement factor so the series stays on
one adjustment basis.

The post-2026-07-22 window was never opened. The failed download wrote
nothing, and the decision was then taken to stay on the existing data. It
remains an untouched holdout, fetchable once as a genuine out-of-sample test.
